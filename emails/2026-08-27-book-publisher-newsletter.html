Real talk.

Every "make an ebook" tool I've tried does one of two things: charges you $29/month, or makes something that looks like a Word doc wearing a suit.

So we built Book Publisher. And made it free.

Here's the whole thing:

  • You write plain Markdown. One file. Headings you already know how to type.
  • Your AI agent does the rest. Claude Code, Codex, whatever you run: cover page, table of contents, page numbers that go i, ii, iii and restart at Chapter 1 like a real book.
  • Because it IS a real book. PDF and EPUB, both.

The part I love: 3 themes, 1 flag.

Same manuscript becomes a corporate lead magnet, an academic essay with margin notes, or a novel with proper footnotes. Change one word in the command. That's the whole redesign.

How to try it (takes 5 minutes):

1. Grab it: bookpublisher.cc4.marketing
2. Drop the folder into your skills directory
3. Tell your agent: "build me an ebook"

That lead magnet you've been "planning" since January? It could exist before lunch.

It's open source (MIT). No account. No server. Your manuscript never leaves your machine.
