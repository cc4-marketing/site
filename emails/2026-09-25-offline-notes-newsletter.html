Save a good article. Open it weeks later. Wonder why you saved it.

The link survived. The thought didn't.

I built Offline Notes to keep them together. It's a Chrome extension for desktop: save the article text, mark the passage that matters, write down why. Read it again without internet, or take your notes elsewhere as Markdown.

  • 100% offline. No account, no server, no sync, no AI, no analytics. Your notes live in your browser and nowhere else.
  • Notes have an exit. Export Markdown that Obsidian, grep, and git can read. A Cornell-style export for highlights is in there too.
  • v1.4.1 protects your work. Bad backups get rejected before they touch your library, and a failed save keeps your typed words on screen.

The free tester is open. Fair warning, it's a real tester: email signup on the page, manual ZIP install, and a separate Chrome profile is the smart way to try it. Three short videos on the page show the whole setup.
